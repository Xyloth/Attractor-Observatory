## 1. Why This Campaign Is Next

The project is still foundation-limited. W3, W4, and W5 are now substantive and hardcode-excised. The remaining production substrate layer is not complete:

- W6 ecosystem is still below the Campaign 007 standard.
- W7 swarm is still below the Campaign 007 standard.
- W8 cognitive/adaptive-control world is absent.
- W9 origins chemistry, W10 hypergraph, W11 quasispecies, W12 symbiogenesis, and W13 multiscale need production reconstruction, not beta-proxy reports.
- K3-K10 calibration remains unsafe until it is backed by real world traces.

Do not build the roof first. Formalism honesty, biology shadow truth, cross-substrate transfer, residual structure, and atlas claims all need load-bearing worlds and world-driven corpora. This campaign therefore finishes the substrate foundation before claim-bearing higher layers advance.

## 2. Campaign Objective

Reconstruct W6 through W13 as production-quality worlds under D7-D14, then replace K3-K10 number-generator corpora with world-driven calibration scenarios whose detectors read traces, not scenario payload answers.

Campaign 008 is complete only when:

- W6R, W7R, W8R, W9R, W10R, W11R, W12R, and W13R are green for causal reasons.
- K3-K10 each meet their real-world scenario minima.

Partial progress is allowed as a working state, but not as a pass.

## 3. Doctrine Updates

These are binding for Campaign 008.

### D15. No Beta-Proxy Credit

Campaign 006 beta-world traces are useful audit scaffolding, but they do not satisfy W6R-W13R. A world gate is green only when the production module under `worlds/<family>/model.py` exports the trace and passes the benchmark.

### D16. Causation Controls Required

Every benchmark must include:

- a positive scenario where the declared mechanism should produce the behavior
- a counterfactual scenario where removing the mechanism should reduce or eliminate the behavior
- a boundary scenario near the transition surface
- a numeric effect size showing the positive differs from the control

Counts, line floors, and "benchmark passed" booleans are eligibility checks, not science.

### D17. Trace-Only Calibration

K-corpus records may store parameters and expected labels, but detectors and calibrators may not read answer fields such as `signal_strength`, `expected_confidence`, `trait_present`, or `benchmark_passed`. They must consume trace state, events, measurements, and invariant records.

## 4. Non-Negotiable Engineering Standard

For every W6-W13 production world:

- no scenario-internal hardcoding under D14
- no benchmark-specific state-writing branches inside step/update/apply/mutation methods
- deterministic replay class declared and verified
- parameter record sufficient to reproduce the run
- positive, boundary, and counterfactual controls
- invariant checks with numeric residuals
- event surface rich enough for substrate-blind projection
- production report generated by code, not hand-written
- tests cover dynamics, controls, trace export, D14 audit, CLI/report path, and at least one failure mode

Substance floors from Campaign 007 remain eligibility floors:

| World | Floor |
|---|---:|
| W6 ecosystem | >=500 simulation-logic lines, >=30 tests |
| W7 swarm | >=500 simulation-logic lines, >=30 tests |
| W8 cognitive | >=600 simulation-logic lines, >=30 tests |

| W11 quasispecies | >=500 simulation-logic lines, >=30 tests |

Line floors never make a gate green by themselves.

### Pillar B: W6 Ecosystem

W6 must become a spatial multi-trophic ecosystem world with resources, patches, interaction matrix, shocks, dispersal, and explicit extinction/recovery behavior.

Benchmarks:

- Lotka-Volterra oscillator: predator-prey phase lag within declared range; period error <= 20 percent against reference.
- May food-web stability: stability transition tracks interaction-matrix spectral radius; ROC AUC >= 0.85.
- Allee collapse: critical threshold bracket width <= 0.15 of carrying capacity.
- Regime shift after shock: hysteresis area > 0.05 and recovery time differs from no-shock control by effect size >= 0.4.

Counterfactual controls:

- remove predators -> oscillator gate fails
- remove shock -> regime-shift gate fails
- flatten interaction matrix -> May transition weakens

Trace events:

`birth_event`, `death_event`, `predation_event`, `resource_flux_event`, `dispersal_event`, `shock_event`, `recovery_event`, `extinction_event`.

### Pillar C: W7 Swarm

W7 must become an agent-based swarm with pheromone/marker fields, local sensing, bounded communication, task switching, trail decay, repair dynamics, and consensus formation.

Benchmarks:

- Trail foraging: food retrieval efficiency >= 1.5x no-pheromone control.
- Division of labour: role entropy in [0.35, 0.85] and task-environment mutual information >= 0.08.
- Collective repair: damaged trail or nest recovers at least 35 percent faster with communication than without.
- Consensus: heading/site agreement reaches >= 0.80 before timeout; no-communication control remains <= 0.55.

Counterfactual controls:

- pheromone disabled
- communication budget zero
- memory decay set high
- random-motion decoy

Trace events:

`movement_event`, `pheromone_deposit_event`, `pheromone_decay_event`, `food_event`, `role_switch_event`, `communication_event`, `repair_event`, `consensus_event`.

### Pillar D: W8 Cognitive / Adaptive-Control World

W8 is missing and must be built as a real world, not as a report layer. It should model embodied adaptive agents with sensors, noisy observations, bounded memory, predictive state, actions that affect the environment, and externalized memory marks.

Benchmarks:

- Homeostasis: controlled variable stays within tolerance at least 70 percent longer than no-controller control under disturbance.
- Anticipation: predictive model reduces one-step-ahead error by >= 25 percent against persistence baseline after learning.
- Externalized memory: mark-based navigation or recovery improves target return/recovery by >= 35 percent against no-mark control.
- Attention budget: performance degrades monotonically under reduced sensing budget, with Spearman rho <= -0.6 between budget and error.

Counterfactual controls:

- memory disabled
- prediction update disabled
- action coupling disabled
- shuffled sensor stream

Trace events:

`sensor_event`, `prediction_event`, `prediction_error_event`, `action_event`, `homeostasis_event`, `memory_write_event`, `memory_read_event`, `external_mark_event`.

### Pillar E: W9 Origins Chemistry

W9 must model pore/surface chemistry with adsorption, desorption, diffusion across a pore graph, energy gradients, surface-catalysed reactions, and protocell-like compartment formation.

Benchmarks:

- Surface-stabilised closure: closure score increases by >= 0.30 with catalytic surfaces vs inert surfaces.
- Transport-limited closure: closure becomes non-monotonic across diffusion rate; interior optimum beats low/high extremes by >= 0.15.
- Gradient-anchored protocell: compartment boundary persistence >= 0.60 and coupled chemistry/boundary correlation >= 0.25.

Counterfactual controls:

- no surface catalysis
- fully mixed pore graph
- no gradient

Trace events:

`adsorption_event`, `desorption_event`, `surface_reaction_event`, `pore_diffusion_event`, `gradient_flux_event`, `compartment_event`.

### Pillar F: W10 Hypergraph Reactions

W10 must stop being a thin W1-adjacent alpha and become a high-order reaction substrate with hyperedge-specific ODE and SSA behavior.

Benchmarks:

- High-order catalytic closure: maxRAF on hypergraph reaches closure confidence >= 0.80 and fails under catalyst-removal control.
- Modular reaction blocks: discovered block modularity >= 0.30 above degree-preserving null.
- ODE/SSA agreement: summary statistics agree within tolerance across matched seeds.
- Hyperedge order effect: reducing all hyperedges to pairwise edges changes motif score by >= 0.20.

Trace events:

`hyperedge_fire_event`, `catalysis_event`, `block_activation_event`, `module_merge_event`, `ssa_jump_event`.

### Pillar G: W11 Quasispecies

W11 must model finite-population sequence evolution with mutation operators, genotype-phenotype landscape, neutral networks, master sequence, drift, selection, and error threshold.

Benchmarks:

- Error-threshold collapse: observed threshold within <= 0.08 mutation-rate units of Eigen-style reference.
- Neutral networks: population traverses neutral graph with phenotype retention >= 0.70 and genotype diversity increasing.
- Evolvable robustness: selected lineages recover fitness after landscape perturbation >= 25 percent faster than random-lineage control.

Counterfactual controls:

- flat landscape
- mutation disabled
- no selection

Trace events:

`replication_event`, `mutation_event`, `selection_event`, `neutral_transition_event`, `threshold_event`, `fitness_recovery_event`.

### Pillar H: W12 Symbiogenesis

W12 must model nested or coupled organisms with resource exchange, partner recognition, cheating, enforcement, engulfment, integration, and inherited compartments.

Benchmarks:

- Stable mutualism: exchange-positive pairs persist >= 3x longer than no-exchange control.
- Cheater takeover: cheater frequency rises under no enforcement and is suppressed under enforcement, effect size >= 0.35.
- Eukaryogenesis-style fixation: nested partner lineage reaches fixation probability >= 0.45 under integration conditions and <= 0.15 under no-inheritance control.

Counterfactual controls:

- exchange disabled
- enforcement disabled
- inheritance disabled

Trace events:

`encounter_event`, `resource_exchange_event`, `engulfment_event`, `cheat_event`, `sanction_event`, `integration_event`, `nested_division_event`.

### Pillar I: W13 Multiscale

W13 must couple at least two substantive world families with explicit upscaling and downscaling operators. It cannot be a synthetic scalar composition report.

Benchmarks:

- Nested closure: motif detected at micro and macro scales, with cross-scale evidence projection agreement >= 0.60.
- Boundary from coarse scale: coarse-scale field or population state alters boundary behavior with effect size >= 0.30 against uncoupled control.
- Scale separation: fast and slow variables show timescale ratio >= 5 and cross-correlation lag in declared range.
- Causal downscale intervention: macro intervention changes micro dynamics beyond null with empirical p < 0.01.

Counterfactual controls:

- upscaling disabled
- downscaling disabled
- time-scale coupling randomized
- one substrate replaced by replay-only trace

Trace events:

`upscale_event`, `downscale_event`, `cross_scale_feedback_event`, `scale_separation_event`, `macro_boundary_event`, `micro_closure_event`, `intervention_event`.

### Pillar J: K3-K10 Calibration Reality

After W6-W13 are substantive, rebuild K3-K10 as world-driven corpora. The scenario payload may declare parameters and labels, but detector input must be trace-only.

Corpus minima:

- K3 memory: >=30 W7/W8 scenarios
- K4 adversarial: >=30 cross-world adversarial scenarios
- K5 ambiguity: >=30 W1/W2/W3/W6 borderline scenarios
- K6 OOD: >=20 worlds or parameter regimes outside detector domain
- K7 composition: >=20 W13 multiscale scenarios
- K8 same-process pairs: >=10 cross-family pairs
- K9 same-appearance/different-process: >=10 cross-family pairs
- K10 non-stationary: >=20 changing-parameter scenarios

Calibration thresholds:

- per-detector ROC AUC >= 0.85 where labels are binary
- ECE <= 0.08 for calibrated confidence
- abstention precision >= 0.80 for OOD corpora
- no detector reads forbidden answer fields
- null margin positive against at least N=200 shuffle/rewire/label-null samples for each calibrated family

## 8. What Not To Do

- Do not use Campaign 006 beta traces to satisfy W6R-W13R.
- Do not satisfy a world gate with a trace count, world count, or file count.
- Do not add `if benchmark == ...` state-writing branches.
- Do not tune a detector by reading scenario answer fields.
- Do not hand-write green reports.
- Do not build FH, BH, CSR, or RS as the main campaign before W6-W13 and K3-K10 are real.
- Do not hide failed benchmarks. Failed causal controls become negative-space entries.
