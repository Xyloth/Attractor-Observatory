## Doctrine Update

No back doors. If a gate can be passed by a degenerate substitution, the gate
fails. Trivial-zero ECE, perfect kappa, zero round-trip loss, basin sampling
inside only-positive regions, and loose dictionary-echo ceilings are failures.

Test continuously and debug at the end too. Each module gets tests as it is
written, then the campaign receives a final adversarial sweep.

Take ownership of the question, not just the gate. Gates are surfaces; the
science is the question behind them.

Future-proof rather than "good enough for now." Choose the right thing when it
earns its weight in code.
