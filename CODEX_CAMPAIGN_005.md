# CODEX Campaign 005: World Ensemble + Search Coupling

## Doctrine

World coverage is not a list of directories. A world counts only if it moves,
emits motif-relevant evidence, preserves declared invariants, and changes what
the Observatory can discover or rule out.

This campaign is complete only when every gate below is evaluated with generated
artifacts. Partial/foundation-slice exit is unavailable for TASK-012.

## Scientific Questions

1. Which missing world families add genuinely new evidence, rather than
   decorative substrate variety?
2. Can search expose basins, novelty, and negative-space structure across world
   families without seeing world identity?
3. Can candidate discovery learn something new when W4/W6/W7/W9/W11/W12/W13 are
   added, or does it collapse back onto existing closure/boundary/field motifs?
4. Can world usefulness scoring tell us which substrates deserve deeper
   investment next?

## Pillar 1: New World Substrates

G1. W4 Morphogenesis alpha emits moving tissue traces with differentiation and
energy/tissue invariants.

G2. W6 Ecosystem alpha emits population/resource/niche traces with nonnegative
population and conservation-style checks.

G3. W7 Swarm alpha emits movement, communication, and environmental marker traces
with agent-count and communication-budget checks.

G4. W9 Origins-chemistry alpha emits adsorption/desorption/surface-catalysis
traces with surface coverage and mass checks.

G5. W11 Quasispecies alpha emits replication/mutation/error-threshold traces with
population and sequence-integrity checks.

G6. W12 Symbiogenesis alpha emits engulfment/resource-exchange/division traces
with nested-mass checks.

G7. W13 Multi-scale composition alpha emits cross-scale flux traces and verifies
micro/macro invariants.

G8. Every new world has a parameter schema, declared determinism class, invariant
checks, and at least two event types.

## Pillar 2: Search That Teaches

G9. Deterministic Latin-hypercube sampler produces reproducible coverage over at
least seven world families.

G10. Novelty search ranks runs by distance from prior evidence and surfaces at
least five non-duplicate high-novelty rows.

G11. Quality-diversity archive covers at least 12 occupied behavior cells and at
least five world families.

G12. Adversarial search targets predicted-empty or weak basins and records both
found and not-found outcomes.

G13. Coverage telemetry includes per-world coverage estimates and Good-Turing
style unseen-cell estimates.

G14. Search is substrate-blind at the scoring layer: feature vectors contain no
world-family identity fields.

## Pillar 3: Discovery, Guidance, and Regression Resistance

G15. Candidate discovery over W1-W13 evidence yields at least eight candidates,
including at least three supported by new worlds only.

G16. At least three candidates have compression gain >0.08 and held-out
recurrence >=0.60 after new-world search.

G17. Negative-space entries are updated with search results from Campaign 005 and
include at least two resolved entries and two open high-effort entries.

G18. World usefulness scoring changes relative to Campaign 004 and identifies at
least three next-investment recommendations with reasons.

G19. A substrate contribution report quantifies marginal information gain by
world family; at least four new worlds contribute nonzero unique evidence.

G20. Prior Campaigns 002, 003, and 004 remain green.

G21. Full pytest passes.
